The raw NHANES .xpt files (data/nhanes = 2017-18, data/nhanes_2015 = 2015-16) are shipped
separately in FairCKD_raw_NHANES_data.zip. Unzip it inside the FairCKD folder.
They are only needed to rebuild data/nhanes_ckd.csv and data/nhanes_ckd_2015.csv from scratch;
the app, tests and saved results all run from the CSV files already included here.
